Call for Expert Reviewers

Contribute your expertise to competitive research funding and help advance research excellence.

Join our pool of expert reviewers and contribute to advancing research excellence.

1

Background

GrantIQ supports competitive research funding through calls for proposals across different science, technology, and innovation priority areas. To do this well, we need subject-area experts to undertake technical reviews of proposals submitted from time to time.

As part of our commitment to sustainable research funding, academic excellence, inclusivity, and intellectual rigor, GrantIQ is constituting a panel of reviewers to contribute their expertise in the proposal review process.

We invite applications from individual experts across diverse geographic regions, institutional affiliations, career stages, and perspectives whose expertise aligns with the relevant thematic areas.

2

Reviewer Requirements

  • Citizenship: Be a Kenyan citizen
  • Qualifications: Academics, researchers, and practitioners with demonstrated expertise in relevant fields
  • Career Stage: Early-career researchers, mid-career scholars, and senior academics are equally encouraged to apply
  • Doctoral Candidates: Advanced doctoral candidates with appropriate subject-matter expertise may also be considered
  • Experience: Expertise in having reviewed other calls will be an added advantage
3

Reviewer Responsibilities

  • Provide a thorough, impartial, and constructive assessment of proposals for funding consideration
  • Evaluate submissions for originality, methodological soundness, and scholarly contribution
  • Adhere to ethical review practices such as confidentiality, data privacy and full disclosure, including conflict of interest
  • Complete allocated reviews within established timelines

Data Protection & Privacy Notice

Your Privacy Matters

GrantIQ is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

Data We Collect:

How We Use Your Data:
